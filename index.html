About The Discipline Engine™

The Discipline Engine is a Christian-founded institution dedicated to cultivating character through stewardship, wisdom, courage, perseverance, service, and lifelong learning. Our general-audience planners and experiences welcome people of every background; explicitly Christian and Catholic formation adventures will also be available.

We believe personal growth should become meaningful action in everyday life.
